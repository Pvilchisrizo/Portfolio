I reviewed other projects, examples, and my notes to clarify the syntax one step at a time. I am trying to make comments on my projects and I feel like it really helps.

The hardest part was the real-time validation — especially making errors appear and clear when the input is corrected.

I knew I wanted to validate the email but didn't know how, so I googled it and learned about Regular Expression testers.

Also I added a live character counter and looked for ways to turn it red when within 50 characters of the 500-character limit.

I checked different websites with contact forms and tried to add some of the features I liked, such as the shake animation and the simulated submission showing "Sending…" and the success message.

I think the biggest win from this project is feeling a little more comfortable reading code and understanding the logic behind it.

It was really nice to organize my ideas and start thinking about how to achieve the results — though sometimes I needed a break because thinking about the whole project at once got overwhelming, so cutting it into small pieces really helped.

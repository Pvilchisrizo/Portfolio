Knowlege Managment — Enablement

As a Technical Trainer at PayPal, I redesigned the onboarding and product training experience for Hyperwallet customer service teams by treating trainees as end users: mapping their pain points, restructuring content around how they actually learned, and validating changes against real performance data. Replacing static manuals with e-learning modules, video tutorials, and step-by-step guides cut training delivery time by 40% and raised pass rates from 60% to 80%. The same user-centered approach scaled across 200+ reps and 150+ new hires in Canada, Mexico, the Philippines, and the US, and carried into a train-the-trainer program I led in Mexico City and the Philippines, and now into a BCIT UX/UI Design Associate Certificate where I'm formalizing that instinct into research and strategy methods.

UX/UI Design in Figma

I earned my User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX) Design Associate Certificate from BCIT in 2025, building a foundation in wireframing, prototyping, and visual design systems in Figma. That training informs how I approach every interface I build, from component layout to accessible color and type choices — including the teal and dark-green palette paired with Atkinson Hyperlegible typography I designed for Soapbox, a full-stack social posting app, to keep the interface both distinctive and easy to read. I bring the same design-first mindset to this portfolio site, translating design decisions directly into the HTML and CSS you're browsing right now.

Web Development

This portfolio site is hand-built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, so every interaction is something I wrote and debugged myself. The contact form on the home page runs real-time client-side validation: it checks name, email format, and message length as you type, tracks a live character counter, and gives inline feedback before submission. It's one part of a broader full-stack skill set I built during my Software Development Bootcamp at UBC Extended Learning & Circuit Stream, where I used JavaScript, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB to build Soapbox, a full-stack social posting app, and a CRUD-based To-Do app backed by Mongoose and MongoDB Atlas.

UX Strategy - The Urban Camp

For this project I redesigned their website to improve navigation and streamline the registration flow, then applied the same research-driven process to a prototype. I mapped user flows, built wireframes, and prototyped the experiences in Figma, iterating on layout and interaction patterns based on usability principles from my BCIT UX/UI Desing coursework. Conducted a mixed-methods usability study of Zara's e-commerce site (5-second tests, moderated think-aloud tasks, competitor benchmarking, and SUS scoring); identified critical search, consistency, and accessibility issues driving a 47.5/100 usability score, and translated findings into redesign proposals for search, navigation, and accessibility.

About Me

I'm a full-stack web developer in Vancouver, BC, with a UX/UI Design Associate Certificate and hands-on experience building projects with JavaScript, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and REST APIs. Before development, I spent 6+ years as a technical trainer at PayPal, turning complex product concepts into clear, user-friendly documentation and today: I care as much about how a feature reads and feels to the person using it as I do about the code behind it. My knowledge management experience spans building and maintaining documentation and training content in Confluence, SharePoint, and Notion, and delivering it through LMS platforms like Cornerstone OnDemand and WorkRamp.
